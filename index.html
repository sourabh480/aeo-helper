    Questions
    
      
    Straight answers, including the uncomfortable ones.
    
      
    
        Will this make ChatGPT or Perplexity recommend my site?
        
    No tool can promise that. AEO Radar checks the technical signals that decide whether AI crawlers can reach and understand your pages, and gives you the files to fix what is missing. Whether an assistant mentions you still depends on your content and reputation.
    
      
    
      
    
        Do AI assistants actually use llms.txt?
        
    It is an emerging convention and major providers have not confirmed they rely on it, which is why it is worth only 10 of 100 points here. Crawler access, structured data and server-rendered content weigh more.
    
      
    
      
    
        Why does the scan say my content is missing when I can see it in the browser?
        
    The scan reads the raw HTML, as many AI crawlers do. If your text only appears after JavaScript runs, it may be invisible to them. The Server-Rendered Content check flags this.
