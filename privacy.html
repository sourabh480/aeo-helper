Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

AEO Radar ("we", "the service") audits public websites for AI search readiness. This page explains what data the service handles.

What we receive

  • Domains you scan. When you enter a domain, our server requests that site's public homepage, robots.txt, sitemap.xml and llms.txt to produce the audit. We do not access anything that requires a login.
  • License key. When you unlock the Fix Kit, the key you paste is sent to our server and checked with our payment provider, Dodo Payments.
  • Technical data. Like most web services, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes your IP address and request metadata to deliver the site and to limit abuse, such as rate limiting.

Anonymous visit counts

To know how many people use the service, each page load (once per browser session) sends a count request. On our side we keep daily totals, and a one-way hash of your IP address and browser details combined with the date, used only to avoid counting the same visitor twice that day. The raw IP address is not stored, the hash cannot be linked across days, and we set no tracking cookies.

Free tester access and feedback

When you claim free tester access we store the domain you claimed it for, and a one-way hash of your IP address to enforce a per-visitor limit (kept for up to 30 days). If you submit feedback we store your rating, comment, the domain, and your email if you choose to enter one. We only publish feedback if you tick the box allowing it, and never with your email. To have your feedback deleted, email us.

Chat assistant

Messages you type into the chat assistant are processed by an AI model on Cloudflare Workers AI to produce an answer. Card-like numbers, long keys and email addresses are removed before the text is sent. Do not type payment details or license keys into the chat. We do not store chat transcripts; we only keep a daily count of messages.

Payments

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, which acts as the merchant of record. We do not see or store your card details. Dodo Payments' own privacy policy applies to the data you give them at checkout.

What stays in your browser

Your browser stores the last scanned domain and your license key in local storage so that the unlock step works after payment. You can clear this at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

Retention

Scan results may be cached for about a minute to reduce repeated requests. We do not build marketing profiles and we do not sell personal data.

Service providers

Cloudflare (hosting and edge computing) and Dodo Payments (payments and license keys).

Your choices

To ask a question about your data, or to request deletion of anything we hold about you, email aeohelper@gmail.com.
