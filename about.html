About AEO Radar

Last updated: 23 November 2026

AEO Radar is a small, independent tool that checks whether a website is technically ready for AI search and answer engines, and helps fix what is missing.

What it does

You enter a domain and the free audit reads the public homepage, robots.txt, sitemap.xml and llms.txt, the way a crawler would. It scores AI crawler access, structured data, indexability, server-rendered content and a few trust signals, then lists the fixes in priority order.

The Fix Kit

For a one-time $29 payment per domain, the Fix Kit generates files for your site: llms.txt, robots.txt rules for AI bots, Organization and WebSite JSON-LD, head tags, a starter sitemap if you have none, and a written report with publishing steps. Payments are handled by Dodo Payments.

What it does not do

AEO Radar cannot guarantee that ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity or any other assistant will mention or recommend your site. The score is our own rubric, weighted toward signals crawlers can verifiably act on, and llms.txt carries only a small weight because major providers have not confirmed they rely on it.
