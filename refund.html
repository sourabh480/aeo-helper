Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The AEO Fix Kit is a one-time digital purchase that is delivered instantly after payment. Because you can preview the free audit and the start of your llms.txt before buying, we handle refunds as follows.

When we refund

  • The kit could not be generated or unlocked for your domain and we could not fix it.
  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase.
  • You contact us within 7 days of purchase and the kit did not do what this site describes.

When we cannot

We cannot refund because an AI assistant did not mention your site or traffic did not change. We do not promise those outcomes, and the audit page says so.

How to ask

Email aeohelper@gmail.com from the address used at checkout, with your order details and the domain you used. Refunds go back to the original payment method through Dodo Payments, usually within a few business days.
